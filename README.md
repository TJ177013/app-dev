# My Favorite Movie: Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse

## Introduction
One of my favorite animated movies is **Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse**, released in 2023. It follows Miles Morales as he travels through different dimensions and meets other versions of Spider-Man.

## Movie Details

- **Title:** Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse
- **Year:** 2023
- **Genre:** Animation, Action, Adventure
- **Main Character:** Miles Morales

## Plot Summary
The movie continues Miles Morales' journey as Spider-Man. He meets Gwen Stacy and discovers the Spider-Society, a group of Spider-People from different universes. He eventually faces a difficult challenge that tests his beliefs and responsibilities as a hero.

## My Favorite Characters

1. **Miles Morales** – A brave teenager who wants to protect everyone.
2. **Gwen Stacy** – A strong and independent Spider-Woman.
3. **Peter B. Parker** – An experienced Spider-Man who guides Miles.
4. **Miguel O'Hara** – The leader of the Spider-Society.

## Why I Like This Movie

I like this movie because of its amazing animation, exciting storyline, and interesting characters. It also teaches lessons about family, friendship, and responsibility.
